I studied computer science and later AI & data science, and now spend my days coordinating IT and experiential learning for students in Toronto — while working toward becoming an independent consultant.

This site is the space where those threads meet: what I'm learning, what I think, and where I've been while thinking it.

## What you'll find here

**Writing** — opinions and half-formed arguments about technology, AI literacy, careers, and whatever else I can't stop thinking about.

**Travel** — a running log of where I've been, less polished than a travel blog and more like a logbook: coordinates, dates, and the one thing worth remembering about each place.
